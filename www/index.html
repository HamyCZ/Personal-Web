Day job

Since 2015 I have been at DXC Luxoft as a Murex Back Office Consultant — first six years in London, then Vienna. As a BO consultant, my focus is on post-trade processing, confirmations, and settlements.

The programmes I went through over the years were for RBS/UB/RBSI on the Treasury side, then NWB and NWM. It is both transformation and migration work — moving books and back-office processes onto the platform, then keeping them correct through cutover and then enabling new business streams on the new platform.
