Interval Training

A free, offline interval timer for boxing, Tabata, CrossFit, floorball, and field tests. 140+ presets. No account required.

Get it on Google Play · App website

I built Interval Training mainly for my own floorball sessions. It is a phone-first timer: presets, custom rounds, and app-timed field tests, all stored on the device.

Sports it covers include boxing and MMA rounds, Tabata and HIIT, CrossFit circuits, and team conditioning. Field tests include 20 m Beep, PACER, Yo-Yo IR1/IR2, 30–15 IFT, and Bronco.

Workouts stay offline. There are no ads during a round or a test. Optional support lives in Settings only. The longer product page and privacy policy live on the app website.

Questions

Is it free?
Yes. The timer, presets, and field tests are free. Optional tips and support ads live in Settings only.
Will ads interrupt a workout?
No. There are no ads during a round or a field test.
Do I need an account?
No. Workouts stay on the device. No sign-in required to train.
